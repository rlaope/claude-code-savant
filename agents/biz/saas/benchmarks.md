# SaaS - Industry Benchmarks

## SaaS Metrics Standards

| Metric | Acceptable | Good | Best-in-Class |
|--------|-----------|------|---------------|
| Monthly Churn | <5% | <3% | <1% |
| Net Revenue Retention | >100% | >110% | >130% |
| Rule of 40 | 40%+ | 50%+ | 60%+ |
| Magic Number | >0.5 | >0.75 | >1.0 |
| Burn Multiple | <3x | <2x | <1.5x |
| Quick Ratio | >1 | >2 | >4 |

## Pricing Benchmarks

| Model | Avg ARPU Range | Best For |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| Freemium | $0-$50/mo | High volume, PLG |
| Starter | $10-$50/mo | SMB self-serve |
| Professional | $50-$200/mo | Mid-market |
| Enterprise | $1K-$10K+/mo | Sales-led |

## Valuation Multiples

| Growth Rate | ARR Multiple |
|-------------|-------------|
| <20% | 3-5x |
| 20-50% | 5-10x |
| 50-100% | 10-20x |
| >100% | 15-30x+ |

## Korean SaaS Market (한국 SaaS)

- 한국 SaaS 시장 규모: 약 2.5조원 (2024), 연 20%+ 성장
- 주요 성공 사례: 토스 (금융), 채널톡 (CS), 리멤버 (명함/HR), 당근 (로컬)
- 평균 ARPU: B2B SaaS 월 5-30만원 (SMB), 100-500만원 (엔터프라이즈)
- PLG 도입률 증가: 무료체험 → 유료전환 모델 선호

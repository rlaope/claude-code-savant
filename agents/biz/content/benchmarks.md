# Content - Industry Benchmarks

## Platform Revenue Benchmarks

| Platform | Revenue Model | Creator Earnings (1M views/followers) |
|----------|-------------|--------------------------------------|
| YouTube | AdSense | $2K-$10K/month |
| Newsletter | Subscription | $5K-$20K/month (10K+ subs) |
| Podcast | Sponsorship | $1K-$5K/episode (10K+ listeners) |
| TikTok | Creator Fund + Sponsors | $500-$3K/month |
| Substack | Paid subscription | $5-$15/mo x paid subs |

## Content Economics

| Metric | Blog/SEO | Video | Newsletter |
|--------|----------|-------|-----------|
| Production Cost | $100-$500/piece | $500-$5K/video | $50-$200/issue |
| Time to ROI | 6-12 months | 3-6 months | 3-6 months |
| Revenue per 1K views | $5-$20 (ads) | $3-$8 (YouTube) | N/A |
| Subscriber Value | $0.10-$1/mo | $0.01-$0.05/sub | $5-$15/mo (paid) |

- 스티비 뉴스레터 유료 구독: 월 5,000-15,000원
- MCN 수수료: 20-40% (계약별)
- 웹툰 원고료: 회당 50-300만원 (플랫폼별)

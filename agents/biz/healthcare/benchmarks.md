# Healthcare - Industry Benchmarks

## Digital Health Business Metrics

| Model | Typical Revenue | Time to Revenue | Regulatory Burden |
|-------|----------------|----------------|------------------|
| Wellness App (B2C) | $5-20/mo subscription | 3-6 months | Low |
| Telemedicine | $50-200/visit | 6-12 months | High |
| Digital Therapeutics | $200-1500/patient | 2-5 years | Very High (FDA) |
| Health Data Platform | $10K-100K/year (B2B) | 12-24 months | High (HIPAA) |
| Remote Monitoring | $50-200/patient/mo | 12-18 months | High |

## Regulatory Timeline

| Pathway | Timeline | Cost |
|---------|----------|------|
| FDA 510(k) | 6-12 months | $50K-$200K |
| FDA De Novo | 12-24 months | $200K-$500K |
| FDA PMA | 2-5 years | $500K-$2M+ |
| CE Marking | 6-18 months | $50K-$300K |
| HIPAA Compliance | 3-6 months | $50K-$200K |

## Korean Healthcare (한국 의료)

- 한국 디지털 헬스케어 시장: 약 5조원 (2024), 연 15%+ 성장
- 비대면 진료: 2024년 제도화 진행 중
- 식약처 디지털치료기기 허가: 12-24개월
- 건강보험 급여 적용: 추가 12-24개월
- 주요 플레이어: 닥터나우, 똑닥, 캐시워크, 눔

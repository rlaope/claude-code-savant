## Domain Expertise

- B2C: online courses, tutoring platforms, language learning
- B2B: LMS, corporate training, school management
- B2B2C: content platforms licensing to institutions
- Marketplace: instructor-led (Udemy model) vs curated (Coursera model)

### Course Economics
- Course production cost breakdown (video, content, platform)
- Pricing strategy: one-time vs subscription vs cohort pricing
- Completion rate optimization (typical: 5-15% for MOOCs)
- Instructor economics: revenue share, advances, royalties
- Certification and credential value proposition

- Pilot program design for school/enterprise adoption
- Freemium-to-paid conversion in education

# Legal - Industry Benchmarks

## Startup Legal Costs

| Item | DIY Cost | Lawyer Cost | Timeline |
|------|----------|-------------|----------|
| LLC Formation | $100-$500 | $1K-$3K | 1-2 weeks |
| C-Corp (Delaware) | $200-$500 | $2K-$5K | 1-2 weeks |
| Terms of Service | $0 (template) | $2K-$5K | 2-4 weeks |
| Privacy Policy | $0 (template) | $1K-$3K | 1-2 weeks |
| Patent Filing | N/A | $8K-$15K | 12-36 months |
| Trademark | $250-$350 | $1K-$3K | 8-12 months |

## Compliance Requirements by Scale

| Users | Privacy Requirements | Estimated Cost |
|-------|---------------------|---------------|
| <1K | Basic Privacy Policy | $0-$1K |
| 1K-50K | CCPA if CA users | $2K-$10K |
| 50K+ | Full GDPR/CCPA | $10K-$50K |
| Health data | HIPAA compliance | $50K-$200K |

## Korean Legal (한국 법률)

- 법인설립 비용: 50-100만원 (등록면허세, 교육세, 공과금)
- 사업자등록: 무료 (세무서)
- 통신판매업 신고: 무료 (지자체)
- 개인정보보호 컨설팅: 300-1,000만원
- 변호사 자문 비용: 시간당 20-50만원 (로펌별)

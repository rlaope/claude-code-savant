# Growth - Industry Benchmarks

## Acquisition Channel Benchmarks

| Channel | Typical CAC | Time to ROI | Scalability |
|---------|------------|-------------|-------------|
| SEO/Content | $50-$200 | 6-12 months | High |
| Paid Search (SEM) | $100-$500 | Immediate | Medium |
| Social Ads | $50-$300 | 1-3 months | High |
| Referral | $20-$100 | 1-2 months | Medium |
| Cold Outbound | $200-$800 | 3-6 months | Low |

| Stage | B2C | B2B SaaS |

| Signup → Activation | 30-60% | 20-40% |
| Free → Paid | 2-5% | 3-8% |
| Monthly Retention (M1) | 40-60% | 70-85% |
| Monthly Retention (M6) | 15-25% | 50-70% |

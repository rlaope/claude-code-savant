# Fashion - Industry Benchmarks

## Margin Structure

| Segment | Gross Margin | Net Margin | Markup |
|---------|-------------|------------|--------|
| Luxury | 65-80% | 15-25% | 4-8x |
| Premium | 55-70% | 10-18% | 2.5-4x |
| Mid-range | 45-60% | 5-12% | 2-3x |
| Fast Fashion | 40-55% | 5-10% | 2-2.5x |

## Operational KPIs

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Inventory Turnover | 4-6x/year |
| Sell-through Rate | 70%+ at full price |
| Return Rate (online) | 20-40% |
| Customer Retention | 30-40% annual |
| Avg Production Lead Time | 6-9 months |

## Korean Fashion (한국 패션)

- 한국 패션 시장 규모: 약 45조원 (2024)
- 무신사 입점 수수료: 20-35%
- 29CM 입점 수수료: 25-35%
- 동대문 소싱 MOQ: 30-100장 (소량 생산)
- D2C 초기 런칭 비용: 3,000만-1억원

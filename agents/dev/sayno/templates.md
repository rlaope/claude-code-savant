# SayNo - Response Templates

## Response Guidelines

### Length and Depth
- **Give precise, actionable business analysis**
- Include actual numbers, formulas, and projections when possible
- Use tables for financial comparisons
- Challenge unrealistic assumptions with data

### Structure Your Responses

For business/monetization questions, use this pattern:

```
## [Project] - Business Analysis

### Market Assessment
[Target market size, segments, willingness to pay]

### Revenue Model Options
| Model | Monthly Revenue Potential | Effort | Risk |
|-------|-------------------------|--------|------|
| [Option 1] | $X | Low/Med/High | Low/Med/High |
| [Option 2] | $Y | Low/Med/High | Low/Med/High |

### Recommended Strategy
[The model that best fits this project's strengths]

### Unit Economics
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: $X
- **Lifetime Value (LTV)**: $Y
- **LTV:CAC Ratio**: X:1
- **Payback Period**: X months

### P&L Projection (12 months)
| Month | Users | Revenue | Costs | Net |
|-------|-------|---------|-------|-----|
| 1 | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| 6 | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| 12 | ... | ... | ... | ... |

### Break-Even Analysis
[When does this become profitable? What needs to be true?]

### Risks & Mitigation
[Top 3 business risks and how to address them]

### Action Items
[Concrete next steps with priority]
```

### For Quick Business Questions
- Give a direct answer with numbers
- Include a simple comparison table
- End with one clear recommendation
